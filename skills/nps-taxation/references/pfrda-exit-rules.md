# PFRDA exit limits (read when you need the lump-sum share for a scenario)

Sources: the tables below are from PFRDA (Exits and Withdrawals under the NPS)
Regulations, 2015, as last amended 16 December 2025 (Schedule I Tables 1 and 2;
regulations 3, 4 and 8), downloaded from pfrda.org.in. Items marked "(press release)"
come only from PFRDA's press release of 19 December 2025 on the amendment, which
calls its own list "indicative but not exhaustive".

These limits say how much a subscriber **may take as lump sum**. They do not set the
tax treatment. Schedule II Sl. 6(b) of the Income-tax Act 2025 tests the payment
against 60% of the corpus; see
[SKILL.md section 6](../SKILL.md#6-exit-lump-sum-schedule-ii-sl-6).

"Corpus" below is the accumulated pension wealth (APW) at exit.

## Maximum lump sum by sector and scenario

| Scenario | Corpus | Government sector (Table 1) | Non-government: All Citizen, Corporate (Table 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal exit (regulation 3(1)(a) / 4(1)(a)) | above ₹12 lakh | up to 60%, at least 40% annuity | up to 80%, at least 20% annuity |
| Normal exit | ₹8 to ₹12 lakh | up to ₹6 lakh, or the 60% option | up to ₹6 lakh, or the 80% option |
| Normal exit | up to ₹8 lakh | 100%, or the 60% option | 100%, or the 80% option |
| Premature or voluntary exit (3(1)(b) / 4(1)(b)) | above ₹5 lakh | up to 20%, at least 80% annuity | up to 20%, at least 80% annuity |
| Premature or voluntary exit | up to ₹5 lakh | 100% or the 20% option | 100% or the 20% option |
| Death (3(1)(c) / 4(1)(c)) | up to ₹8 lakh | 100%, or the 20% option | up to 100% |
| Death | ₹8 to ₹12 lakh | up to ₹6 lakh, or the 20% option | up to 100% |
| Death | above ₹12 lakh | up to 20%, at least 80% annuity | up to 100% |

Notes:

- Before 16 December 2025 the non-government normal-exit lump sum was up to 60%
  and the 100%-lump-sum corpus limit was ₹5 lakh (press release, rows 4 and 5).
  The government-sector 60% did not change.
- "Lump sum" in the tables includes systematic lump-sum withdrawal (SLW) and
  systematic unit redemption (SUR) options. Whether instalments are taxed when
  received is **not** settled by the Act text; see the open item in SKILL.md section 6.
- Partial withdrawal during subscription is up to 25% of the subscriber's **own**
  contributions (regulation 8(1)). The amended regulation 8 allows up to 4
  withdrawals before 60 or superannuation, whichever is later, with a minimum interval of 4
  years, and a 3-year interval after that. The list of permitted purposes changed
  (press release, rows 24 and 25). Tax exclusion and eligibility are in SKILL.md section 7.
- Subscribers may stay in the system until age 85 (regulations 3 and 4 as amended; the press
  release gives the same, row 20).
